How to use

▶Play & tempo

Tap the big orange Play button to start the beat, and tap it again to stop. A white outline moves across the grid to show where you are.

Tempo is in BPM (beats per minute). Drag the slider (40–240), use − / + to nudge it by 1, or tap Tap in time with a song and it will match the speed.

🥁Sounds (Kick, Snare, Hi-hat)

Each tab is a different drum: Kick, Snare and Hi-hat. Each one has its own pattern, and all of them play together. The grid shows whichever tab you're on.

Switch tabs anytime. The beat keeps going. The dot on each tab blinks when that drum plays.

▦The grid

The grid is 4 bars, each with 16 steps (sixteenth notes). That's 64 steps, and then it loops.

The steps come in groups of four. The first step of each group, a slightly lighter square, is the beat: 1, 2, 3, 4. Holding your phone upright, each bar is two rows of 8. Sideways, each bar is one row of 16.

👆Adding hits & volume

Tap a step to add a hit. Tap it again to change how loud it is:

  • Loud (full)
  • Medium
  • Low
  • Barely there (a "ghost" note)

After "barely there" it goes back to loud. When the beat is stopped, you'll hear each tap at its new volume.

✋Deleting a hit

Press and hold a step for about half a second to remove it. It shrinks while you hold. Let go early and it counts as a normal tap.

🎚Volume for each drum

The slider under the tabs sets the overall volume of the drum you're on. For example, turn the hi-hat down so the kick and snare stand out. Each drum remembers its own level.

🎲Random

Random makes a new pattern for the tab you're on, and it's built to sound like a real groove instead of noise. The kick favors the main beats, the snare lands on 2 and 4 with soft ghost notes, and the hi-hat keeps time.

Bars 2 and 3 are small variations on bar 1, and bar 4 changes a bit more, like a fill. Press it again for a new one, or Undo to go back.

⋯Bar menu: copy, random, clear

Tap a bar number (1–4) on the left to open its menu:

  • Copy this bar to Bar 2/3/4 or All. It copies volumes too.
  • All sounds: tick this to copy, randomize or clear kick, snare and hi-hat together. Leave it off to change only the drum you're on.
  • Random bar / Clear bar: the same as the main buttons, for just that bar.

Tip: build one bar you like, then copy it to All.

↶Undo & Clear

Undo steps back through your last 50 changes: taps, deletes, random, copy and clear.

Clear empties only the drum you're on. The others keep playing.

📱iPhone tips
  • No sound? Turn up the volume. On recent iPhones it plays even with the silent switch on.
  • Leaving the page (home screen, lock, another app) stops the beat. Tap Play when you come back.
  • Turn the phone sideways to see all 4 bars at once.
  • To make it feel like an app, tap Share → Add to Home Screen in Safari.
  • Your pattern isn't saved yet, so reloading the page starts fresh.
